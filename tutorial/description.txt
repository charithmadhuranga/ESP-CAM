VisionCam Pro: Build a Local-First ESP32-CAM Security Camera (RTSP, Home Assistant, SD Recording)

Turn a cheap AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM into a standalone security camera that keeps working without the internet or the cloud. VisionCam Pro is free, open-source (MIT) firmware built with native ESP-IDF and PlatformIO. It has a React web UI, records AVI clips to microSD, detects motion, streams over RTSP and integrates with Home Assistant over MQTT.

In this video we cover the hardware and wiring, the firmware architecture, the video and RTSP pipeline, storage and motion detection, Home Assistant integration, and first-time setup and security.
