Setting up a new camera takes just a few minutes.

With no saved network, the camera starts its own setup access point. Join it, open the setup page, and enter your Wi-Fi name and password.

You can also set Wi-Fi over USB. Send one line on the serial port with the network name and password, separated by tabs, and the camera saves them and reboots.

The password is never echoed back, and the logs only ever show its length.

On first sign-in, you must replace the default admin password before anything else works.

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes. Sessions live only in RAM, in an HTTP-only cookie, and every change request also needs a CSRF token.

Every stream, snapshot, recording, and settings endpoint requires sign-in. RTSP has its own login, and the camera stores only a hash of that password.

To connect Home Assistant, open Integrations, enable RTSP, and press Copy. Type the RTSP password, and the camera builds the ready-to-paste stream address without saving it.

And keep the camera on your local network. It is built to be private by default, not to be exposed directly to the internet.
